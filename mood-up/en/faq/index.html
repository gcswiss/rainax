FAQ

Good to know.

The most important answers about Mood Up. Your question isn't here? Write to us.

What is Mood Up?

Mood Up is your radar for parties nearby. The interactive map shows you the most popular parties, bars and clubs around you, live and without endless searching.

Does Mood Up cost anything?

No, Mood Up is free for users.

Is Mood Up available for iPhone and Android?

Yes. You'll find the app on the App Store as “Mood Up - Party Radar”, and it is also available for Android.

What can I filter by?

With the Smart Filter you choose from many categories, for example type of venue, music, age and date.

What does Mood Up cost for bars and clubs?

Getting started is free: venue on the map, creating parties, chat function for users and collecting followers. With Pro for CHF 49.90 per month you also get statistics (views and clicks), messaging your followers and posting stories.

Can I plan ahead?

Yes. Choose today, tomorrow or a date in the calendar and plan your night right now.

Can I see the vibe beforehand?

Yes. You check the vibe and the music style before you head out.

How does the guest chat work?

Once you're at the venue, you join its guest chat and chat right away.

How does my venue get on Mood Up?

Write to us at info@mood-up.ch. After that you add your events yourself, quickly and easily, and appear on the live party map.

What happens to my data?

Mood Up complies with the strict Swiss data protection law. You'll find details in the Privacy policy.

Questions?
Write to us.

We'll reply as quickly as possible.
