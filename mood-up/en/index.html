Good to know.

What is Mood Up?

Mood Up is your party radar. The interactive map shows you the most popular parties, bars and clubs near you, live.

Does Mood Up cost anything?

No, Mood Up is free for users.

What can I filter by?

With the Smart Filter you choose from many categories, for example type of venue, music, age and date.

How does my venue get on Mood Up?

Write to us at info@mood-up.ch. After that you add your events yourself, quickly and easily.
