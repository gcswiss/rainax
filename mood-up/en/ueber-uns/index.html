About us

From Wil.
For every party night.

Mood Up grew out of conversations with the hospitality industry. Guests look for experiences that suit them, bars and clubs fight for visibility. We bring both together.

Our story

The idea for Mood Up came in 2023 from direct conversations with bars and clubs. Instead of searching Google, Maps and Instagram, one look should be enough: where's the action tonight, and is it right for me?

From the first idea through design and development to launch, the app was created in Wil SG. Started in Wil, built for all of Switzerland.
